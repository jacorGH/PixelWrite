How it works

  • Drag with one finger to pan. Pinch to zoom; twist two fingers to turn the view.
  • Build: pick a piece, then tap the ground or the top of something. Tap a wall to hang windows, doors and grates flush against it. Ring walls, arches, ramps, domes and posts make wells, gates and towers.
  • Land: drag to raise, dig, level or smooth the ground, or paint ground tiles. Use two fingers to move the view while sculpting.
  • Select: tap pieces, then move, raise, turn, resize or copy them. Arrows follow your view.
  • Paint: choose a texture and tap any face. Painting a face inside a prefab updates every copy.
  • Pixels: draw 16×16 textures. Tap a face while here to open its texture. Palette edits recolor everything. Turn on Shell layers to grow grass, fur or moss out of a texture, one blade per pixel.
  • Edit shape (in a selected piece's panel): turns any piece, such as a pipe, into an editable shape. Tap faces or points, then move, grow, shrink, turn on any axis, extrude, inset, flip, merge or delete them, and paint single faces. Make a prefab from the result.
  • Prefabs: select several pieces, make a prefab, then place copies from Build.
  • Play (top bar) switches from creating to playing. The bottom bar becomes Bag, Skills, Combat and Moves; tap Create to go back to building.
  • Combat: tap a rat or goblin to fight. Pick a style in Combat to train Attack, Strength or Defence; eat food to heal. Creatures drop things on the ground: tap to pick them up. Bury bones for Prayer.
  • Things you drop stay on the ground for a few minutes, even if you leave and come back. Days and nights pass while you play; the sun button in the top bar holds it at day or night instead.
  • Chests, barrels and crates hold your things: tap one to open it, then tap items to move them in and out. Any piece can be made a container from its In Play setting.
  • Make any mechanic (Create → Game): Skills adds new skills that level like the rest. Talk builds branching conversations for people (or signs and objects), with choices that check conditions and run actions. Rules make things happen: when an item is used, an object is tapped, an area is entered, a creature dies, a skill levels, something is gathered or made, or a new game starts, check conditions (items, flags, numbers, skill levels, quests, area, night, chance) and run actions (give or take items, XP, flags, numbers, heal, hurt, teleport, start quests, teach spells, open a conversation, summon creatures, play a sound). Old Tom's chat, Alchemy and healing draughts are examples.
  • Stacking and the bank: materials, food, bones, logs, ores and buckets stack in one slot. Use Sort and Arrange under your bag to tidy it. The bank booth on the village path stores unlimited stacks; the altar east of the village restores prayer.
  • Prayer: bury bones to train it, then switch prayers on in the Magic tab for defence, strength, accuracy, faster healing, or half damage. They drain prayer points.
  • Skill guides: tap a skill to see what each level unlocks.
  • Share your game: Create → Game → World → Export playable game saves one HTML file that opens straight into the game with a title screen and no editing tools. Back up and load world files there too.
  • Tool belt: axes, pickaxes, nets and the tinderbox hang on your belt instead of taking bag space, and you can see them on your character. Tap one on the belt to put it in your bag, drop it, or sell it in a shop. Worn gear doesn't use bag space either.
  • Piles: tap things on the ground to see everything lying there; take one or take it all.
  • Map: the Map tab in Play shows the whole world, its areas, people (gold if they have a quest for you), creatures and portals. Tap it to walk there. Music and Sounds switches live there too.
  • Backpacks: make a satchel, pack or rucksack from animal hides at the workbench and wear it for more bag space. Hides come from rats, wolves, bog beasts and trolls, which also give leather armour; the forge makes bronze and iron armour.
  • A bigger world: Game → World → Generate grows the map around your village into Greenvale, Whisperwood, the Ashen Dunes, Mirefen and Frostspire, with roads, harder creatures further out, and the Sunken Crypt, a dark dungeon reached through a cave in the Frostspire. Paint your own areas in Land → Area.
  • Magic: the Magic tab lists your spells. Attack spells fire when you attack; Mend heals, Glow lights the dark, Stoneskin protects, Levitate lets you fly over anything, Recall takes you home. Read spell tomes to learn more. Bows shoot from range.
  • Branching quests: choices in conversation change what happens next and set flags that open other quests. Vex the wanderer, east of the bridge, has a choice for you.
  • People, quests and shops: tap someone to talk. Farmer Bess, Dorin the smith and Old Tom have work for you; Dorin also runs a shop. Quests shows what you're doing. Coins come from creatures, quests and selling.
  • Game tab (Create): make crops that grow over time, people with lines, quests with steps and rewards, shops, armour, recipes that take time to make, and what a new game starts with. Place people with the Person piece and the start with the Player start piece.
  • Mining and smithing: mine the copper, tin and iron rocks and the glowing crystal with your pickaxe; smelt bars and forge tools and swords at the forge by the mine.
  • Items → Stations: make your own stations (an anvil, an oven, a loom…), place them and reshape their bodies. Items → Resources: make your own gatherables (a silver rock, a berry bush…), choose the tool, skill and what they give. Recipes tie it together: ingredients in, anything out, at any station.
  • Items (Create): make new axes, weapons, shields, food or materials, draw their icons in Pixels, and give them recipes. Make them at the workbench in Play.
  • Your own creatures: build one from pieces, make it a prefab, then add it under Prefabs → Creatures and place a Creature spawn in Build.
  • Walking: tap to walk there. Tap water to fish or fill a bucket, or tap the well. Walk in through a cottage door and the roof lifts away while the walls fade. Your catch and Fishing level show in the panel; tap an inventory slot for options.
  • Tap the campfire with raw fish to cook them; Cooking levels mean fewer burnt fish. Tap cooked fish in your inventory to eat.
  • Tap a tree to chop it with your axe for logs; it falls after a few and grows back. Tap logs (or the tinderbox) to light a fire where you stand, then cook on it.
  • Swimming: walk into water from a shore, the pond or a canal's open end, and you swim. Swimming XP comes with every stretch you swim.
  • Farming: tap tilled soil to plant wheat seeds; it sprouts, grows and turns gold. Harvest the grain, grind it at the quern into flour, mix flour with a bucket of water into dough, and bake it on a fire into bread.
  • Canals and bridges: Land → Canal digs a stone-lined channel and floods it as you drag. Build has canal pieces (straight, bend, T, cross, end) and a Bridge with stairs or ramps and railings you can switch on and off.
  • Land → Water: tap a dip to fill it with water up to where it would spill over; tap water to drain it. Dig a hollow first to make a pond.
  • Any piece can be given a Walk action from its Select panel: fishing spot, water source, cooking fire or nothing.
  • Land can also grow the map: add land to any side, a few tiles at a time.
  • In Edit shape, pick Faces, Edges or Points. Points and edges are picked near your finger. Swipe pick lets you drag across many; quick buttons grow the pick, take everything linked, everything facing the same way or with the same texture, or exactly what the last extrude made. With faces picked, tap a texture to paint them.
  • The grass button in the top bar turns shell layers on or off for slower phones.
  • { } opens the text editor. Pick what to edit (the world, the selection, a prefab, terrain, textures), type, and apply. Quotes around names and trailing commas are optional, and // comments are allowed. Tap a piece while it’s open to jump to its text.

Keyboard: wheel zooms, right-drag turns, Q/E turn the view, R turns the selection, arrows move it, Delete removes it, Ctrl+Z undoes.
