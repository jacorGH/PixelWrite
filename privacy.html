Privacy Policy

Pixelwright by Jacob Cooper

Effective November 24, 2026

In short: Pixelwright has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not collect your personal information. Your worlds and progress stay on your device. When you play together with other people, your device connects directly to theirs.

What stays on your device

Your worlds, game progress, settings and the name you choose for multiplayer are saved in the app's storage on your device. They are not sent to the developer. They are removed when you delete the app (or clear the website's data in your browser). Files you choose to export or back up go wherever you save them.

Playing together

When you host a world or join someone else's, your device connects directly to the other players' devices (peer-to-peer, using WebRTC). Because of how these connections work:

  • To find each other, devices use the free PeerJS connection service (operated by the PeerJS open-source project) and public STUN servers (such as Google's). These services see your IP address and a temporary connection code while the connection is set up. The developer does not run or receive data from them.
  • Other players in the same world can see your IP address, the name you chose, where your character is, your chat messages and items you give them. The host's device passes these on to the other players.
  • When you host, a copy of your world is sent to the players who join.
  • Chat is sent directly between players. The developer does not receive or store chat.

You never have to use multiplayer. Mute hides a player's messages, and the host can remove players.

Reports

If you report a player, your email app opens a message to the developer containing the reported player's name, the world code, your chosen name, your reason, and that player's recent chat messages. Nothing is sent unless you send that email. Reports are used only to review abuse and enforce the community rules, and are deleted within 90 days of being resolved.

Things loaded from the internet

If the app can't find its built-in copies, it may load its 3D and connection libraries from cdnjs.cloudflare.com and unpkg.com, and its fonts from Google Fonts. As with any web request, those providers receive your IP address. The app works with fallback fonts if Google Fonts is unavailable.

Children

Pixelwright is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because multiplayer chat is with other people, parents should decide whether children may use it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
